An Organization for Single Parents

Parents Without Partners (PWP) is a single parent support group for all single parents and their children. PWP is a 501.C3 educational organization promoting single parents and their children.

Who we are

Parents Without Partners, Inc. is an international non-profit and non-sectarian organization of single parents — widowed, divorced, and never married — raising their children alone.

The challenge

Providing a nurturing family life is often complicated by established community patterns. Resources to assist single parents in meeting their special circumstances are essential.

Our conviction

Through working together, exchanging ideas, and mutual understanding, we've established PWP to further the common welfare and well-being of our children.
